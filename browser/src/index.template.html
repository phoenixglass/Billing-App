Unbilled Billing Processor

Choose your billing file and it's processed right here, on this computer.

🔒Everything happens on this computer. This page works offline: your file is read by your browser and is never uploaded anywhere. The finished reports are saved to your Downloads folder.
No file chosen Must be .xlsx with the date in the filename as MMDDYYYY. You can also drag the file here.

Staff assignment follows the standard daily schedule automatically, based on the date in the filename: Self Pay always bills every day to CB; for Insurance rows, everything that goes to Jasmine and Cathy (Professional, IOP, Day Treatment, and billable Programming/e-care rows) is sorted alphabetically by Client and split exactly in half, every day. The halves alternate daily: on Day A Jasmine gets the A-M half and Cathy the N-Z half; on Day B they swap. All Insurance PHP rows go to Melissa in the Masters report (no individual report is generated for her).
